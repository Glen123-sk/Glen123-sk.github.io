Funeral Support System

How Activations Work

When a household experiences a bereavement, the community rallies. Here is how the system processes support with dignity and transparency.

I
Admin Activates Event

Admin logs the bereavement and initiates a community support event from the control center.

II
Households Notified

All 2,000 households receive an SMS and in-app notification of the contribution requirement.

III
Wallet Deductions

Contributions are automatically deducted from household wallets or queued for manual payment.

IV
Receipts Generated

Each household receives a digital receipt and the transaction is recorded permanently.

V
Support Distributed

Funds are released to the bereaved family's designated account within 24 hours of collection.
